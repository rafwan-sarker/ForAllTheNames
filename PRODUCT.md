# For All The Names

register: brand

## Product purpose
Every proper noun Drake has ever said in his own songs (and his guest verses on popular features): places, athletes and teams, musicians, brands, women, other people. Counted, ranked, mapped, and traceable to the exact bar. Built from Genius lyrics with Claude extraction.

## Users
Drake fans and rap-nerd group chats. Scene: someone in bed at 1am, phone brightness low, scrolling bars and screenshotting a ranking to send to the group chat; secondary, a desktop visitor who lands from a shared link and goes down a rabbit hole.

## Brand voice
Nocturnal, braggadocious, intimate. Liner notes of a platinum record, gold foil on black. Funny in the copy, serious about the data.

## Anti-references
- drizzy.world (niko draca): white Swiss minimal, left album sidebar, map as the centerpiece. We must not look like it.
- Generic SaaS dashboards: KPI tiles, card grids, hero metrics.
- Neon "music app" gradients.

## Design (v2, 2026-11-02)
One screen, few words. Visual left (map or name cloud), list and lyrics right. One readable sans font. No hero, no paragraphs, no stat tiles.

## Strategic principles
- The lyric is the hero. Every number links back to the bar that produced it.
- Rankings first, map second.
- Niche references are the payoff; make deep cuts easy to find (search).
- No em dashes in copy.
